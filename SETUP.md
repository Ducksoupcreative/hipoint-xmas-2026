# Your Christmas email signature – setup

Hi Virya, here's how to add your Christmas signature. It takes about two minutes.

## Outlook on your Windows PC

1. Open the signature link in **Edge or Chrome**.
2. Click just above **OFFICE MANAGER**, then drag down to the end of the **Christmas Closure** line, so only the signature is highlighted (not the whole page).
3. Press **Ctrl + C** to copy.
4. In Outlook, click **New Email**, then **Signature** › **Signatures…**
5. Click **New**, name it **Virya Prum Christmas 2026**, click into the big box and press **Ctrl + V**.
6. Check the logo with the lights, the icons and the Santa are showing. They load from the web, so you need to be online.
7. Choose it as the default for **New messages** (and replies, if you like), then click **OK**.
8. Send yourself a test email. The signature should line up with the left edge of your message text.

## Outlook on the web

Settings (cog) › **Account** › **Signatures**. Paste it the same way, straight from the browser. If the pictures disappear, copy it again from the browser, not from Word or another email.

## Gmail

Settings (cog) › **See all settings** › **General** › **Signature** › **Create new**. Paste it from the browser, then click **Save Changes** at the bottom of the page. Send a test email to check it.

## After Christmas

When you re-open on 7 January, switch the default back to your everyday signature.

If anything looks indented, the pictures are missing, or it looks different on your phone, stop and let Keir know. Don't keep pasting it from a different app.
